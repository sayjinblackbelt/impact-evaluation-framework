# Requirements and sizing

Before defining a solution, identify requirements that determine scope, timeline and effort.

## Key questions

- What must the evaluation demonstrate?
- Who will be evaluated and at what volume?
- What period will be analyzed?
- Which materials and data already exist?
- Is there a baseline or comparable history?
- Who collects, validates and analyzes data?
- What technology infrastructure exists?
- Who will use dashboards and reports?

## Sizing

**Scope + time + volume + complexity + responsibilities + technology.**

Build the technical and financial proposal after this diagnosis.
