# Overview

The **Impact Evaluation Framework** treats evaluation as an integrated system, from understanding the problem to producing evidence for decisions.

## Flow

Understanding → Model → Indicators → Instruments → Data → Analysis → Management → Final evaluation.

## Purpose

Support teams in designing evaluations for projects, programs and social initiatives without making the methodology dependent on a specific technology.

## Principles

- Understand before measuring.
- Measure what matters for decisions.
- Distinguish activity, output, outcome and impact.
- Respect data limitations.
- Prioritize quality and traceability.
- Make technology serve methodology.
- Protect privacy and data governance.
