# Evaluation model

## Logic chain

**Resources → Activities → Outputs → Outcomes → Impacts**

The logic model connects inputs, implementation, outputs and expected changes.

## Theory of change

It explains how and why activities are expected to produce outcomes and impacts, including assumptions and conditions.

## Methodological caution

An observed change does not, by itself, demonstrate that a program caused it. The strength of conclusions must match the evaluation design and available data.
