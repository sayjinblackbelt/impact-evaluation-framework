# Data and privacy

This public framework must not contain personal data, sensitive data or confidential institutional information.

## Allowed

- synthetic data;
- fictional examples;
- generic structures;
- properly anonymized or sanitized data when appropriate.

## Do not publish

- participant names or contacts;
- personal documents or identifiers;
- raw client or organizational data;
- credentials and keys;
- information that identifies a real project without authorization.

Sanitization is a mandatory step before publication.
